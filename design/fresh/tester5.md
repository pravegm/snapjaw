## What I think it is and how to play
- A calm timing game.
- Bugs drift onto a flytrap and sit on gold drops ("When it's sipping… tap to snap!"). You tap once to shut it.
- The trick is patience: wait until several bugs are sipping, then bite them all ("QUAD ×4 · +128").
- Bees and wasps are the "bad dots": eat one and you lose snaps.
- You start with about 12 snaps, a big bite gives one back ("+1 snap"), and it ends when you run out.
- Back-to-back big bites build the "×1.5 → ×2" chain. That's my favourite part, like making a square in Two Dots.

## What I loved
- **The open trap right after a bite (21–23).** The juice sparkles, the snap flying back to the row, the "+1 snap"
  tag: very satisfying.
- **The nectar drops in little groups of three (12, 17, 35)**, so you know where a crowd will form.
- **The Bug Book's silhouettes with hints** ("Comes at dusk", "Spare one to fill this page"). That's a goal list, and
  I like goals.
- **The colours.** The night (17) is gorgeous.

## What confused me
- **What grows the chain?** I worked it out from the numbers, but the game never says so. Does a 1-bug bite break
  it? (A "×1.5 held" would tell me, if I ever saw it.)
- **The lesson showed 10 snaps (05–09), then my first game had 12 (10).** Small, but puzzle players count.
- **The same slot says "Now!", "Bee! Wait…" and "Last snap!"** (24, 11, 18). Fine, but in 15 two messages land on
  top of each other.
- **"Wait too long and a snap wilts" (09).** I never saw it happen, so I don't know how long "too long" is. In Two
  Dots I always know my moves left. Here a hidden timer could feel unfair.
- **There are no levels.** It's endless runs plus a daily. I'd want a reason to come back beyond beating my score:
  the ranks ("200 more bugs to Gnat Gobbler", 02) help.
- **Daily (25):** the date runs under the pause button.

## Would I pay £1.99?
**Probably, for the Daily Bog** if it's as calm as it looks. Two Dots is free, but its ads drive me mad, and "no ads,
no purchases" (30) matters. I'd want a short video first, to know it isn't a twitchy reflex game.

## Would I play again?
Yes, a daily-habit game for me: one Daily Bog with my coffee, and a few Feed runs to fill the Bug Book.

## "Feels finished": 7 / 10
Polished and gentle. It loses points for the overlapping messages (15, 25) and for rules I had to guess (the chain,
the wilt timer).
