# Fresh player 9: "Sam", mobile games journalist, writing an "iPhone Duo games, first wave" roundup

*Sam has played Fold Smasher (free; snap shut to trap bugs crossing both halves), ChompDuo (free; the inner screen is a
monster mouth that the fold shuts, a toy with no goals) and Foldfish (free with IAP; the hinge is the rod). Sam saw only
the R4 frames in story order, as a press kit, with no build and no docs.*

## What I think it is and how to play
- A premium (£1.99) score-attack game built on the Duo's fold. The two halves are the two lobes of a Venus flytrap,
  and you "snap it shut a little" (03, 31) to bite.
- The twist on Fold Smasher's verb is **choosing**:
  - wait for a crowd ("Now!", 11 and 24);
  - never bite a bee or a wasp ("Bee! Wait…", 12; "Stung!", 15);
  - snaps are a budget that big bites refund ("+1 snap", "+2 (full)");
  - dither and one wilts ("Too slow! −1 snap", 34);
  - a chain multiplier and a day cycle change the cast;
  - plus a Daily, a 16-bug Bug Book, ranks and 12 Game Center badges.
- It's the only one of the four with a real game loop and a long tail. ChompDuo is a toy, and Fold Smasher is a reflex
  smasher with IAP.

## Confusions / what I'd flag in a review
- **There is no Duo in the press kit.** Every frame is phone-shaped (19.5:9) and says "Tap to snap" (10, 38). For a
  game whose pitch is "the fold is the jaw", that's the first question I'd ask the developer. Fold Smasher's store page
  at least shows a hand on the hinge.
- **31 is broken** (black block, a rotated diagram, stacked traps). If that's in the build, it's in my screenshots.
- **The art splits in two:**
  - the open trap in play (10, 35) is gorgeous and clearly a flytrap;
  - the icon is excellent, a cute open jaw with a fly peeking out;
  - the Results/cover/badge trap (20, 26, 32) is a different drawing, a red speckled cap on a green bowl, and it reads
    as a watermelon wedge;
  - the post-bite frames (14, 39) are a dark maroon blob.

  Pick one trap.
- **Small truthfulness bugs** a reader would screenshot:
  - "Out of snaps" over a counter of 1 (19) or 2 (40);
  - "Hungry… bite!" with a bee in the trap (33).
- The dusk grade (16, 37) is olive and muddy next to the lovely night (35).
- I can't judge the feel, the CHOMP or the haptics from stills, and that's the whole point of a fold game.

## Would I pay £1.99?
As a Duo owner, yes. It's the most *game* of the first-wave Duo titles I've seen, and "no ads, no purchases" is a
selling point next to three free-with-IAP competitors. The price anchor is the problem: Fold Smasher is free and its
one-line description sounds the same. The store page has to show the bee and the crowd in the first screenshot, or
people will file it as "the paid Fold Smasher".

## Would I play again?
For the roundup, yes: the Daily is a reason to come back, and I'd want to see whether the night ever gets unfair. I
can't promise beyond a week from stills alone.

## "Feels finished": **7 / 10**
It feels like a finished phone game with a Duo hook I haven't been shown working. The polish is high and the
systems are complete, but the press kit has one broken screen and two art styles for the hero.

## The verdict I'd publish
> **Snapjaw** turns the Duo's fold into a Venus flytrap's jaw and, unlike Fold Smasher's free-for-all, makes you
> *wait*: for a crowd of flies, for the bee to leave, for the snap that pays for itself. It's the most complete game of
> the Duo's first wave and worth £1.99 to owners, though we'd like to see the hinge as charming in the hand as the bog
> looks on screen.
