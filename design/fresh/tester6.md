# Fresh player 6: "Tom", 34, a product designer and iPhone Duo early adopter

*He saw only the frames, in this order: the icon, the badges, Home 01–03, pose 31, cover 32, lesson 04–09, play
10–24, 33–35, Results 20, 26–27, Daily 25, Bug Book 28–29, Settings 30. He read no docs.*

## What I think it is and how to play
- A Duo-native flytrap. The two halves of the Duo are the two lobes, and you clap the phone a little shut to bite
  (03: "Snap it shut a little"; 31: "The two halves are the trap. Snap it a little shut to bite").
- Score attack with a snap budget: refunds for crowds, penalties for bees and wasps, a chain multiplier, and a day
  cycle that changes the cast.
- The concept is the best Duo idea I've seen: the hinge really is a midrib.

## What I noticed as a designer
- **Every frame is phone-shaped (19.5:9).** I never saw the Duo's near-square 951 × 669 canvas or the hinge band. I
  can't tell whether the trap, the HUD and the hint fit the real device. If this is a Duo game, show me the Duo.
- **The fold-as-input is undersold.** The diagram on Home (03) is the size of a stamp and sits on the trap's cilia.
  The lesson says "tap to snap" (04, 08). If I bought it for the hinge, the first minute should be all about the hinge.
- **The open trap (10, 17)** is lovely: the clam lobes, the seam notches, the fine cilia, the stem, the stippled
  glands. The peach band at the margin is the one off note: it tips it back toward fruit.
- **The shut trap (13, 14, 18)** is physically off. A closed Venus flytrap from above shows the *outside* of the
  leaves (green), with the cilia interlaced. Here you see the red inner faces bulging, zipped up the middle.
  Graphically it reads as a pod, or something ruder. And the eaten bugs are still sitting on top of it (13).
- **The icon:** cute and clean, but it's a different illustration from the game (a 3/4 cup with a navy fly, against a
  top-down trap with grey flies). The store page will look like two products.
- **The badges:** a coherent medallion system with good tiers. But two pairs are near-duplicates (`bite5`/`feast`,
  `spare50`/`nobees`), and the trap-only ones (`daily`, `monarch`, `score10k`) read oddly in isolation.

## Would I pay £1.99?
**Yes, if the store page has a real-Duo video** of the clap catching a crowd. As a showcase for the hardware I'd pay
more. Without that, it looks like a nice tap game, and there are free ones.

## Would I play again?
For the hinge feel: yes, daily for a couple of weeks. The Daily Bog and the leaderboard would keep me.

## "Feels finished": 6 / 10
The open-trap art and the menus are store-grade. But a Duo game I've never seen on a Duo, a hero bite frame that
reads wrong, and four clipping or collision bugs say "one more round".
