## What I think it is and how to play
- You're a Venus flytrap. Bugs land on your leaf and sip the little gold drops, and you tap (or, on that folding
  phone, close it a bit) to snap shut.
- Wait until loads of bugs are on it, then bite them all at once. That's the big score ("QUAD ×4", "FEAST ×5").
- Don't bite bees. They have a white circle round them, and the red "Bee! Wait…" bubble tells you.
- The little green pill things at the bottom right are your snaps. The card in 09 drew a box round them, which
  helped. Big bites give snaps back, and you lose one if you wait too long ("Too slow! −1 snap").
- It's endless until you run out. Then you get a score, which they call protein.

## Confusions
- **Why "protein"?** It's funny once, but I kept reading it as a stat, not my score.
- **The chain chip** (×1.5, ×2, ×2.5). The hint in 13 ("Chain ×1.5! Keep the big bites coming") explained it, but
  then in 42 it says "×2 held" and I didn't get what "held" means. Did I keep it, or is it frozen?
- **33:** a bee with its white circle is *in* the trap, and the bubble says "Hungry… bite!". So do I bite or not?
- **19:** it says "Out of snaps" but the counter says 1.
- **31** looks like a glitch: a black block, two traps on top of each other, and sideways text. I assumed it was a
  loading frame.
- **The dark red shape after a bite** (14, 39) didn't look like the plant any more. In 39 I honestly thought "plum".
- **The trap on the Results and the badges** looks like a watermelon slice with eyelashes.
- I liked that the lesson is only four steps and has a Skip.

## Would I pay £1.99?
Probably not on my own phone, because I don't have a Duo and I've never paid for a game: Wordle and Royal Match are
free. If I *did* have the folding phone, then yes, £1.99 is fine for "no ads, no purchases" (30). Royal Match
*constantly* asks me for money, and I'd pay just to not be asked.

## Would I play again?
Yes, the **Daily Bog**. "Today's bog is ready" is my Wordle brain exactly: one go a day, same for everyone. I'd want
a share card ("Daily Bog 28 Oct: 4,786 · 90 bugs · 7 bees spared") so I can send it to my group chat. Without that it's
a game I'd forget in a week. The Bug Book with its jokes ("Has never once wiped its feet") is the collection itch
Royal Match scratches.

## "Feels finished": **7 / 10**
It looks like a real, polished game: bright, cute bugs, nice big numbers. Three things looked broken or contradictory:
the pose card, "Out of snaps" over a 1, and "Hungry… bite!" next to a bee. Fix those and it's an 8.
