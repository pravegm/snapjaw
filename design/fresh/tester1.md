# Fresh player 1: "Sam", 30, owns an iPhone Duo, never reads instructions

*Saw only the frames, in this order: lesson 04–09, play 10–19, 33, 34, results 20, 26, 27, Home 01–02, Bug Book
28–29. Read no docs.*

## What I think the game is
A big pink plant-mouth (or a watermelon? I honestly thought watermelon for a second) sits on a pond. Flies land on
it and I tap to make it chomp them. More flies in one chomp means more points, called "protein". Don't eat the bees.
The little watermelon slices in the corner are... lives? They go grey as the game goes on, and when they're all grey
"Your trap is tired" and it's over.

## How to play (as I understood it)
- Wait for flies to sit on the pink part ("when it's sipping"), then tap.
- Try to get two or three at once.
- If there's a bee, wait.
- If you wait too long, it says "Hungry… bite!" and you lose one of the slices.

## What confused me
- **The skip-and-tap lesson told me to tap.** I have a Duo, and nothing in these screens told me I could fold it.
  "Tap to snap" everywhere. I'd never have tried closing the phone.
- **When it bites, the whole screen turns into a giant green eye** with a red pupil and spikes (13, 17, 18, 19). I
  thought I'd zoomed out, or it was a different mode. It took me a few frames to see it was the "mouth shut" picture.
- **"Last snap — make i…"** is cut off. Make it what?
- **"wilted −1"**: what wilted? I didn't see anything change except the number going 12 → 11.
- **The slices.** I didn't know they were snaps until the card said "Snaps run out". The number next to them (11)
  plus 11 slices is the same thing twice.
- **"Chain ×1.5"** appears out of nowhere at the top. No idea what I did to get it.
- **Protein** as the score word is funny, but I didn't get it until Results.

## Would I pay £1.99?
Maybe. It looks polished and cute, the bugs are adorable, and the Bug Book jokes made me smile. But from these
screens it looks like a tap game I could get free. If the store page showed me folding my Duo to bite, I'd pay, because
that's the reason I own the Duo.

## Would I play again?
A few times, mostly to fill the Bug Book. My first run (20) ended fast: 14 bugs and done.

## "Feels finished": 6 / 10
Pretty and clean menus. The bite picture and the cut-off hint make it feel unfinished.
