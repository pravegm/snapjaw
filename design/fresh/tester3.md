# Fresh player 3: "Dev", hardcore mobile gamer, has played Fold Smasher

*Saw only the frames, in this order: lesson 04–09, play 10–19, 33, 34, results 20, 26, 27, Home 01–02, Bug Book
28–29. Read no docs.*

## What I think the game is
A timing-and-greed score attack: a single-screen trap, a bug spawner, and a snap budget. You spend a snap per bite.
3+ bugs refund one. Bees cost you, and idling costs you ("Hungry", "wilted −1"). Score is points × bug count × a
chain multiplier ("TRIPLE ×3 · +66", "FEAST ×6 · +360", "Chain ×1.5"). Runs end when the budget hits 0. Daily seed,
Bug Book collection, ranks ("Fly Catcher → Gnat Gobbler").

## Versus Fold Smasher
Same family (bugs plus a clap), but this is about *waiting*, not mashing: the budget and the "Wait for a crowd… now"
line make it a push-your-luck game. That's a real difference, if the hinge feel is good. The frames I saw are all
"tap to snap", so I can't tell whether the fold is the input. If it's tap on the Duo too, it's a casual tapper.

## What confused me or worries me
- **Depth.** Every frame shows "Chain ×1.5". I never saw ×2 or higher except "×3 (4 bites)" on one Results (26).
  Whether the chain is the skill expression or noise isn't clear.
- **The bee penalty.** Results 27: "Best bite 4 bugs · 1,032" and "Bees spared 1" on a first run, and "You caught a
  Golden Scarab." If a bee was in that 1,032 bite (I can't tell), it should hurt more than it seems to.
- **The run length.** The first Results (20) is 14 bugs and 809. That's a 1-minute run. Fine for a daily, short for
  the main mode.
- **The HUD duplicates itself.** "11" next to 11 icons. And the icons are tiny watermelons.
- **The bite frame** (13, 17–19) is visually noisy: there's a second ring of ghost teeth on a green band, and you can't
  tell which bugs got eaten and which escaped. I want an instant read of "got 3, missed 2".
- "Last snap — make i…" is truncated, which is sloppy for a paid game.
- Hunger fires while 9 bugs are sitting there (33, 34). Fine as a no-input test, but if the timer is short at night,
  it'll feel like a clock, not a choice.

## Would I pay £1.99?
Not from these screens alone; Fold Smasher is free. I'd pay if a store video shows a real Duo snap catching a 6-bug
Feast with good haptics, and the leaderboard and daily are there (Game Center is in Settings, which is good).

## Would I play again?
The Daily Bog, yes, if the leaderboard is live. Feed maybe a week.

## "Feels finished": 5 / 10
The menus and Results are store-quality. The core moment (the bite frame), the economy readout and the copy clipping
aren't there yet.
