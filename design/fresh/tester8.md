# Fresh player 8: "Margaret", 60, a grandmother of three who plays solitaire and the odd jigsaw app

*She saw only the R4 frames, in story order (icon, badges, Home, pose, cover, lesson, play, Results, Daily, Bug Book,
Settings), on a laptop screen, with nobody explaining. She read no docs.*

## What I think it is and how to play
- It's a carnivorous plant, a Venus flytrap, like the one my grandson had on the windowsill. Insects come and drink
  the honey drops. You make the plant shut on them by tapping, or by folding one of those new bendy phones.
- "When it's sipping… tap to snap!" (04) was clear. "Two flies. Get both in one bite." (05) was clear. "Never eat a
  bee. Wait…" (07) was clear, and I liked that the game is on the bees' side.
- You have a number of goes (the green beans at the bottom with the number beside them). Catching three or more gives
  you a go back. If you dither, you lose one.
- At the end it tells you how you did, and you fill in a little book of insects.

## Confusions
- **The words.** I didn't know:
  - "Protein" (why not "points"?);
  - "HUD on the left" (30);
  - the chain thing with the paperclip "×1.5";
  - "Feast ×5 · +915" (I understood the 915).
- **The text is small** on the play screens. The number of goes in the corner (bottom right, "10") is tiny next to the
  big score at the top.
- **31** was broken: a black square, the pictures on top of each other, and the "138°" on its side. I'd have thought
  my phone was faulty. And I don't know what 138 degrees means for holding a phone.
- **After a bite** the plant turns dark red and round (14, 18, 39, 42), and I couldn't see the green edge or the
  little spikes any more. It didn't look like a flytrap, more like a big plum.
- **15 and 40:** "Stung!" and "Your trap is tired" at the same moment. Was I stung *and* tired? Was it the wasp's
  fault? In 40 it says out of snaps but the counter says 2.
- **33:** it tells me to bite while there's a bee in the circle. I wouldn't dare.
- The dusk pictures (16, 37) look muddy yellow-green, as if the colour's gone wrong, not like evening.

## Would I pay £1.99?
For the grandchildren, yes, if I had the folding phone. "No ads, no purchases, no data collected" (30) is exactly what
I look for before I let them play on mine, and it's gentle: nobody gets squashed, the bees are spared, and the bugs
have silly faces. I wouldn't buy it for myself.

## Would I play again?
A few times, with the grandchildren. They'd love the Bug Book jokes ("Came for the banana. Stayed for the nectar."). On
my own it's a bit quick and twitchy for me: the night ones look fast. A slower, relaxed mode with no running out
would keep me.

## "Feels finished": **6 / 10**
The pictures are lovely: the bog, the lily pads, the bugs with big eyes. But I saw one screen that looked broken
(31) and a couple of moments where the game seemed to say two things at once. The writing is too small in play.
