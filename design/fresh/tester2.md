# Fresh player 2: "Mia", 12

*Saw only the frames, in this order: lesson 04–09, play 10–19, 33, 34, results 20, 26, 27, Home 01–02, Bug Book
28–29. Read no docs.*

## What I think the game is
You are a Venus flytrap and you eat bugs! It's like feeding a pet monster. Flies and beetles land on your mouth, you
tap and CHOMP, and it says TRIPLE or FEAST. Bees are good guys so you don't eat them ("Bee! Wait…"). Wasps sting you.
At the end you get "protein", which is like how full your plant is.

## How to play
Tap when lots of bugs are on the pink bit. The more bugs, the bigger the number (FEAST ×6 got 360!). Don't tap when
there's a bee with a white circle. The circle with dashes in the lesson showed me which bugs to look at, which was
helpful.

## What confused me
- **Why is my plant a watermelon?** The little icons in the corner are definitely watermelons. And on the "Your trap
  is tired" screen it's just a watermelon with spikes.
- **The green eye thing** when it bites. It looked like the plant was staring at me. A bit creepy, but I kind of liked
  it?
- **The card with lots of words (09)**: "Every snap costs one. Three bugs in one bite give one back…" I skipped it.
  I'd want it to just show me.
- **"Chain"**: what is a chain? There are no chains.
- **"wilted −1"**: I didn't know plants could wilt in the game. Did I do something wrong?
- **"Hungry… bite!"** when there were loads of bugs on it (33). Why didn't the plant just eat them?
- The night one (15, 17) made the plant purple. Is it a different plant?
- The mosquito looks like a spider.

## Would I pay £1.99?
I'd ask my mum. It's cute and there are no ads (it says so in Settings), and that's good. But my friends play free
games. Maybe, if there were more plants or hats for the plant.

## Would I play again?
Yes! I want to find all 16 bugs in the Bug Book. The jokes are funny ("Has never once wiped its feet."). I want to
see the Golden Scarab because Results said "Lucky trap!".

## "Feels finished": 7 / 10
It looks like a real game from the shop. The cut-off words ("make i…", "Golden Sca…", the bee joke going off the
screen) look like mistakes.
