# Snapjaw scorecard: R5 (baseline round)

*Panel run on 28 Oct 2026 against `design/SCORECARD.md`. This is the first round scored on the card, so it is the
baseline every later round is compared with.*

## Evidence

  and 12 badges. They were shot on the iPhone 18 Pro (2622 × 1206, tap control). Captions are in `ci/scenarios.txt`.

  store-duo, store-placements). It shows "PROTEIN" and the plum release.

juror 8, UX 8, feel 8, Duo 7 (provisional), icon 8, store set 5.

## 2. One reason per cell

### 1. ADA juror
- **D1 8.** One hero trap, from the icon to Results (26) to the cover (32). The release is crimson and glossy, and the
  bugs are charming. But dusk and night are muddy sage (16, 17, 37), which stops a 9.
- **D2 8.** The rule set reads from the frames alone: gold sip ring, white bee halo, red wasp ring.
- **D3 7.** Wait / judge / bite is clean, but every run is the same single screen.
- **D4 8.** The closed-jaw silhouette (13) and the open crimson release (14, 39) are proper hero frames.
- **D5 8.** Halves as lobes is the most literal Duo idea in the category. It has not been seen on an R5 Duo.
- **D6 7.** It's worth £1.99 as a crafted object, but the page doesn't prove it yet.
- **D7 7.** The Daily, the Bug Book and 12 ranks are there, but there's no streak and no share.
- **D8 6.** The icon is excellent. The store set is the R4 build: PROTEIN, a plum release (P02, P04) and olive dusk.
- **D9 7.** Bugs sit on the midrib (41) and next to the HUD (10, 11). "Stung!" is drawn over the wasp (40).

### 2. Game-UX / onboarding lead
- **D1 8.** The HUD, banners and cards agree. The chain chip, the "+1 snap" pop and the hint all crowd the
  right-bottom corner (14, 22).
- **D2 8.** The lesson teaches by doing in 4 steps, and 09 points at the snaps. But messages double up:
  - "Never eat a bee" and "Bee! Wait…" (07);
  - "×2 held" twice (42);
  - "Stung!", "free!" and the hint all at once (43).
- **D3 7.** The push-your-luck moment is legible (11: eight flies and "Bee! Wait…"). There are no goals beyond score.
- **D4 7.** The pops are good. A FEAST ×6 (39) is the same size as a QUAD (13).
- **D5 7.** The phone lesson skips the pose step. The only Duo lesson frame says "Step 2 of 6" (R4).
- **D6 7.** The first minute is strong. The store doesn't show the lesson's clarity.
- **D7 7.** Results names the next rank (26, 27) but no next bug or next goal.
- **D8 6.** P10 says "Step 4 of 4, now tap!" over a Duo pitch. The captions mostly hold, but P05 and P06 don't.
- **D9 7.** 05 and 06 are the same frame. The 05 and 17 captions don't match what's shown.

### 3. Game-feel designer
- **D1 8.** The bite cycle looks juicy. The juice blobs read beige rather than nectar gold (14, 25).
- **D2 8.** Good and bad are clear at a glance: gold versus white versus red rings.
- **D3 8.** The economy is tuned: Sprout taper, wilts only while a catchable bug is in, chain up to ×5 (39).
- **D4 7.** No hit-stop and no screen-wide moment on a big bite. Caught bugs linger by the splats (14). Sound and
  haptics are unheard.
- **D5 8.** The partial 22° snap and relative rest angle are real hinge design. Touch snaps at d = 0.09 s catch

## 3. Below target: the 3 changes that raise each dimension most

### D1 Design (8.0 → 8.5)
1. **Scene.** Make dusk and night read at a glance:
   - **Dusk:** peach sky and a warm top-left glow.
   - **Night:** deep blue, with fireflies and crickets inside the trap.

   Today 16 and 37 are sage-grey, 17 is "night" but still dusk, and 35's night has only houseflies.
   *Frames 16, 17, 35, 37; store P05, P06.*
2. **Scene.** Keep a clear zone around the HUD:
   - the bog housefly parked beside SCORE (10, 13, 21, 24, 38, 41);
   - the bumblebee under the pause button (11);
   - the fly over PROTEIN (duo-01).
3. **Scene.** Keep the lime rim on the tired trap (19). Make the bite juice nectar-gold sparkle, not beige blobs
   (14, 25).

### D2 Ease (8.0 → 8.5)
1. **App/UI.** One message at a time. The lesson card and the hint never repeat each other (07). One "held" label,
   not two (42). In a stung bite, show the pop or the hint, not both plus "free!" (43).
2. **App/UI.** Label the snaps like the score: a larger numeral with "snaps" under it (10, 18). Explain the chain
   chip once, at the first chain step: "Big bites in a row multiply" (13, 14).
3. **App/UI.** Gate "Hungry… bite!" off while any halo overlaps the trap edge (33, REVIEW-5 P2-1). Pick the Duo
   lesson's step text on the first hinge sample (REVIEW-5 P1-4).

### D3 Fun (7.0 → 8.5)
1. **Core.** Give each run three small objectives, for example "Catch a dragonfly", "Spare 5 bees" and "Reach ×3".
   - Show them on Home and tick them on Results (02, 26).
   - This is what the puzzle and casual players are missing.
2. **Core + Scene.** Make the day arc change the play, not only the tint. Night bugs (firefly, cricket) must
   dominate the night trap (35 shows houseflies). Dusk must bring visible dragonflies and moths (16 does; 37 doesn't).
3. **Core.** Pace the late night. Telegraph the end (a "last light" banner, a rising bee count) so expert runs don't
   drift past 6 minutes (REVIEW-5 P2-5; 39 is 8,462 at wave 6+ with 14 snaps).

### D4 Satisfaction (7.0 → 8.5)
1. **Feel + Scene.** Make a FEAST of 5 or more an event:
   - 60–80 ms hit-stop;
   - a pop scaled by bug count;
   - the snaps row refilling glyph by glyph;
   - a stronger double haptic.

   Today 39's FEAST ×6 looks like 13's QUAD.
2. **Scene.** Caught bugs visibly gulp into the midrib within the 0.12 s closed frame. In 14, the fruit flies still
   sit beside the splats, which blurs "caught" against "escaped".
3. **Feel (founder).** Run real-Duo checks 2, 8 and 9 (REVIEW-5 §6): the CHOMP against the clop, the heavier wasp
   wobble, the snap's first-try feel. This half of D4 can't be scored from frames.

### D5 Duo-first (7.5 → 8.5)
1. **Store/CI.** Land `duo5`: the R5 build at 951 × 669. Gate it on:
   - SNAP, REARM, CREEP and CLOSE-PAUSE;
   - no pause at 95°;
   - too-flat at 178°;
   - `LESSON-HINGE`;
   - the pose card, the cover, Results, and lesson steps 2–3.

   *(duo4 only.)*
2. **App/UI.** Centre the cover-display card and fill it, with "score" (duo-04 is off-centre with an empty right half
   and says "protein").
3. **Store/CI.** Close the P01/D01 book to a visible ~140° so the fold reads at search-thumbnail size. Use the Duo
   reserve "The teeth follow your hands" (24-style closure) as a Duo frame.

### D6 Willingness to pay (7.0 → 8.0; players 3 of 5 → 70%)
1. **Store/CI.** Regenerate the whole store set from R5 (`store2`). No page converts at £1.99 while it shows a
   different, older build (store1, all frames).
2. **Store.** Sell what the free toys lack in frames 1–3: choosing and bees.
   - Use 11's real moment for P02 or P03: eight flies, a bee, "Bee! Wait…".
   - Put "No ads. No in-app purchases." in a sub-line (it's allowed; it isn't a price).
   - Fix the "×4" claim to ×5.
3. **Core + App/UI.** Add run objectives (D3 #1) and the Daily share (D7 #1), so the page can honestly say "a new
   goal every run, one Daily for everyone". That answers the puzzle player's "endless only?".

### D7 Replay (7.0 → 8.0)
1. **App/UI.** Daily Results with **Share** (captured and tested), plus a Daily streak on Home: "3-day streak" next
   to "Today's bog is ready" (02, 25; REVIEW-5 P1-1).
2. **Core.** Run objectives (D3 #1), with a lifetime tally on Results.
3. **App/UI.** Results names the next Bug Book target, for example "Something with four wings comes at dusk",
   using the Book's own silhouettes and hints (26, 27, 28).

### D8 Store appeal (6.0 → 8.5)
1. **Store/CI.** Build `store2` from R5 under REVIEW-5 §4's rules:
   - no "protein";
   - crimson open release in P02 and P04;
   - a real dusk P05;
   - P06 recaptioned or re-shot with crickets and a moth;
   - P09 as Daily Results with Share;
   - P10 at "Step 4 of 4" with Duo words in D10.
2. **Store/CI.** Mask the Dynamic Island pill from phone captures (store P06; also 36).
3. **Icon.** Lighten the two dark "nostril" dots on the front lip (icon about 480,710 and 595,690). Re-shoot P01 so no
   bee halo is cut by the screen edge next to "Now!" (P01/D01).

### D9 Polish (7.0 → 9.0)
1. **Scene.** Enforce the midrib and edge rules:
   - nothing rests within 20 pt + r + 16 pt of the midrib (41's fruit flies at the base, 24, and 11's gnats);
   - no halo or bug cut by a screen edge (11's bee, 33's bee, 39's dragonfly).
2. **App/UI.** Pops never cover their subject:
   - "Stung!" sits above the wasp's ring, not on the wasp (15, 40, 43);
   - "Too slow! −1 snap" clears the flower (34);
   - the hint bubble doesn't cover bugs in the trap (43).
3. **Store/CI + App/UI.** Make the words consistent across app, copy and CI:
   - "Score on the left" (30) against "HUD on the left" in `metadata.md` §11 ask 4 and the review notes;
   - stale `ci/scenarios.txt` captions (05 = 06 duplicate; 17 "night" captured at dusk; 23, 30 and 26 "protein");
   - the Game Center score10k detail "protein" (REVIEW-5 P1-3).
