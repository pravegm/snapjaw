Researched 27 Oct 2026. Sources: foldhunt.com (games list plus the full /iphone-duo/ index), duodiscover.com (games category plus app pages), App Store listings, iThinkDiff, TechCrunch, Notebookcheck, Xataka MX, Apple Newsroom, appleworld.today, 9to5Google/Android Central (Android foldables).
Anything marked [unverified] comes from search snippets or third-party summaries and has not been checked against an App Store listing.

Limits: PhoneArena's "best iPhone Duo apps & games" roundup returned 403, and no r/iPhoneDuo thread was indexed. There may be very new titles that neither directory has picked up yet.

## TL;DR

| # | Concept | Collision level | Main collider(s) |
|---|---------|-----------------|------------------|
| 1 | Venus flytrap (halves = lobes) | **HIGH (mechanic), none (theme)** | **Fold Smasher**: bugs move across both halves, you snap shut to trap them. No flytrap or plant-themed hinge game found. |
| 2 | Jaws / mouth / chomp | **MEDIUM** | **ChompDuo**: inner screen is a monster mouth and folding shuts the jaws. It is a novelty toy with no goals. |
| 3 | Fishing, hinge as rod | **HIGH** | **Foldfish**: "the hinge works as the fishing rod", launched week of 25 Oct |
| 4 | Drawbridge traffic control | **CLEAR** | None found on Duo or Android foldables |
| 5 | Crane / tower stacking by hinge angle | **CLEAR** | None found. Only Duometry/Obtuse (angle matching) are adjacent. |
| 6 | Snap/catch reflex | **HIGH** | **Fold Smasher** (catch moving targets by snapping shut). Clap: Film Slate is a non-game clap app. |

**Bottom line for the flytrap:** the core verb (things move across both halves, you snap the phone shut on them) shipped about 4 days ago as Fold Smasher. Fold Smasher also overlaps Squish. It is a free novelty with IAP, has no ratings yet, and there is no plant, no bees and no partial-close trap. To stand apart, the flytrap has to lean on what Fold Smasher does not do:
- a partial snap: the lobes meet at an angle and you never press flat
- avoid-the-bee risk, so not every snap is good
- the real "two touches before it closes" trigger-hair biology
- digestion and growth
- a premium £1.99 game structure rather than a toy

## 1. Venus flytrap / carnivorous plant

**Direct mechanic collision: Fold Smasher (Ouail Bellal)**
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fold-smasher/id6824005343
- Subtitle: "Fold Your Phone to Smash!"
- Free with nine $0.99 IAPs. v1.0.0 and v1.0.1 were both about 4 days old on 27 Oct, which puts launch around 23 Oct. Requires iOS 27.1. No ratings yet.
- Mechanic, from the App Store text: bugs and other creatures move across both screens. Closing slowly freezes a target, and a quick clap shut finishes it. Reopening shows the splat, broken armour or confetti where the target was trapped.
- Targets: Emerald Beetle, Crimson Ladybug, cockroach, mouse, spider, sports balls, Cybertruck, balloon, robot. Each one begs for mercy with voice lines.
- DuoDiscover lists it as "Built for Duo", added 24 Oct 2026, not device-tested: https://duodiscover.com/apps/fold-smasher
- It is not on foldhunt.com's games list as of 27 Oct.
- What it lacks: no plant or trap fantasy, no hazards to avoid (bees), no partial-angle catch, and no progression beyond a lifetime smash count. It is a toy with IAP skins.

**Theme (no hinge mechanic): flytrap apps on the App Store**
- Venus Flytraps (hap Inc.), a tap-to-feed growing sim: https://apps.apple.com/app/id650748941
- FlyTrap (Glinkie Games), a flappy-bird game: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flytrap/id6563140200 (the US page returned 404 when fetched [unverified current availability])
- Fly Trap (Martin Blampied, 2014), one-tap, avoid the flytraps: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fly-trap/id873464804
- Flytrapped (MassDiGI), a 2D platformer: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flytrapped/id1591106533
- Fly Trapper! (Rollic), eat bugs to evolve: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fly-trapper/id6502290019
- Android "Venus Flytrap" casual tap game on QooApp: https://apps.qoo-app.com/en/app/6558
- itch.io jam games: Rush, Fumble, and Chomp (https://hireddivas.itch.io/rush-fumble-and-chomp) and Muscipula (https://neal-waken-games.itch.io/muscipula) [unverified content]

**Android foldables:** no flytrap or fold-to-snap game found. Foldy Bird (flappy) and Kami (origami) are the only well-covered hinge games there:
- https://9to5google.com/2026/01/03/flappy-bird-clone-for-foldables/
- https://www.androidcentral.com/gaming/android-games/someone-made-foldy-bird-and-its-a-glorious-way-to-destroy-your-foldable

**Verdict:** the theme is open on Duo and the mechanic is taken by a very fresh, low-depth toy. That is still viable if the flytrap's partial-angle snap and bee-avoidance are central, and if the marketing never says "fold shut to smash".

## 2. Jaws / mouth / chomp

**ChompDuo (Brian Corrieri)**
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chompduo/id6823172460
- Subtitle: "Silly eyes. Hungry monsters."
- Entertainment category, free, with an "All Monsters" IAP at $0.99. v1.0 on 19 Oct and v1.0.2 about 3 days ago. One rating.
- The cover screen shows eyes. Opening the phone turns the inner display into a mouth, and folding shuts the jaws. The jaws track the hinge angle, and the bite comes with sound and haptics.
- Six monsters: Cartoon, Softie, Organic, **Mimic**, Classic, Pumpkin.
- There is no game loop, scoring or objective. The listing describes it as a novelty.
- Press coverage: Xataka MX, 26 Oct 2026: https://www.xataka.com.mx/celulares-y-smartphones/iphone-duo-tiene-aplicaciones-que-no-tendrian-sentido-otro-celular-preparar-sandwiches-alimentar-monstruos
- **Note for Hush:** ChompDuo ships a "Mimic" mouth skin. It is a toy and not a chest game, but the word is now used in the Duo space.

**Monster, Ink.** (Bitrig hackathon project), via Notebookcheck: https://www.notebookcheck.net/Made-for-iPhone-Duo-These-apps-show-what-Apple-s-foldable-can-do.1416967.0.html
- It is a focus timer. Fully opening wakes a cat-monster that wrecks your city. There is no chomp mechanic.
- It is close to Hush's "don't open it or it wakes" idea, but it is a productivity app. Its App Store status is [unverified].

**Non-hinge chomp games:** CHOMP!!! (finger in mouth), Chompy Chomp (chameleon), Big Bite, Hungry Crocs, Chomp Catch. None use the hinge.
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chomp/id861917111
- https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1065199621
- https://apps.apple.com/app/chomp-catch/id6762557989

No crocodile, clam, whale or Pac-Man-mouth hinge game turned up on Duo or Android foldables.

**Verdict:** "close the phone as a mouth" exists as a toy (ChompDuo). A goal-driven eating game is open, but it collides with both ChompDuo and Fold Smasher on the verb.

## 3. Fishing, hinge as rod

**Foldfish (Jan Poncik)**
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/foldfish/id6824988199
- Coverage: https://www.ithinkdiff.com/foldfish-iphone-duo-fishing-game/ (25 Oct 2026)
- Free, with IAP coin packs at $0.99, $1.99 and $3.99, plus Foldfish Gold at $3.99. v1.0 shipped the week of 25 Oct, and v1.0.2 added more than 30 languages.
- Mechanic: unfold to load the cast, tap to throw, hold to reel, and avoid the other fish. The article says the hinge "works as the fishing rod".
- Not yet listed on foldhunt or duodiscover.

**Verdict:** taken. Foldfish reels by holding the screen rather than pumping the hinge, so a "pump the fish" variant is technically distinct. Even so, the headline "hinge is your rod" is already owned.

## 4. Drawbridge traffic control

- Nothing found on the Duo directories, in press, or on Android foldables.
- "Draw Bridge" App Store games are touch-only puzzles where you draw a line bridge: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1664325526
- Closest hinge neighbours:
  - Uphill (slope follows the hinge)
  - Marbles Unhinged / Fold: Gravity Puzzle (fold tilts the floor)
  - Fold Party's "Safe Cracker" (hinge as a dial)

**Verdict:** clear.

## 5. Crane / tower stacking by hinge angle

- Nothing found. Crane stackers such as Skyward Towers and Tower Stack Neo are touch-only:
  - https://apps.apple.com/us/app/skyward-towers/id1580321353
- Smash It! on foldhunt is a physics game about knocking stacks over, adapted for the Duo, with no hinge control: https://foldhunt.com/games/smash-it/
- Adjacent games: Duometry/Obtuse (angle matching) and the Angle Meter / Protractor utilities.
  - https://www.ithinkdiff.com/duometry-iphone-duo-angle-guessing-game/
- The ~5° hinge accuracy makes precise placement a design risk.

**Verdict:** clear.

## 6. Snap/catch reflex (net, swat, clap)

- **Fold Smasher** is the direct hit. See section 1.
- **Clap: Film Slate** is a utility, not a game, on foldhunt (simulator verified, 23 Oct). Shutting the Duo claps it, with each half acting as a stick.
- Timing-on-close games in other themes:
  - **Toastie**: close to toast, and opening too early undercooks it. Coverage: https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/24/these-clever-apps-show-off-the-iphone-duos-potential/
  - **WaffleKing**: pour, fold shut to bake, unfold in time: https://foldhunt.com/games/waffleking/
  - **Exactly Half**: close to chop.
- No butterfly-net or mosquito-clap hinge game found. Swatter games on the App Store are all touch-only: Catch The Housefly, SwatzZapz, Whack A Fly.

**Verdict:** "catch moving things by snapping shut" is taken (Fold Smasher). Selective catching, where some things are good and some bad and the timing is in the air, is not taken.

## Name check (flytrap concept)

"Exact" means a game titled exactly or almost exactly this word. Searches were web searches of App Store listings, not an exhaustive store search.

| Name | Crowding | Notes |
|------|----------|-------|
| Snap | **Very crowded** | Snap! Deluxe, Snap - Classic Card Game, SNAP! Fun Card Game, Snap GG and others, plus Marvel Snap mindshare. Unsearchable. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/snap-deluxe/id617406914 |
| Chomp | **Crowded** | CHOMP!!!, Chomp Catch (2026), Little Chomp, Chompy Chomp, Osmo Words Chomp, and **ChompDuo on Duo itself**. Also Apple's former "Chomp" search engine. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/chomp/id861917111 |
| Flytrap | **Crowded** | FlyTrap (Glinkie), Fly Trap (Blampied), Flytrapped, Fly Trapper!, Venus Flytraps. Generic and descriptive. |
| Gulp | **Moderate** | No exact iOS "Gulp" found [unverified]. Close: Galactic Gulp (frog, $2.99), Gulpy Is Hungry (frog eats flies), gulper.io, and an OrangePixel "Gulp!" on Android. "Frog eats flies" is a close thematic neighbour. |
| Snaptrap | **Light** | One 2011 game, "Snap Trap!" (8 Bit Grenade); no current listing found, probably delisted [unverified]. https://toucharcade.com/community/threads/8-bit-grenade-presents-snap-trap-official-trailer.105273/ |
| Snapjaw | **Clean** | No game found under this exact name. Near: Blockjaw, Smash Jaws, Jaw Runner 3D. |
| Dionaea | **Clean (games)** | No game found. It is also the name of a well-known honeypot security tool. It is hard to spell and pronounce, and a weak fit for search. |

**Recommendation**
1. **Snapjaw**: clean, punchy, and reads as both "snap shut" and "jaws". It fits the studio's one-word, verb-ish naming (Squish, Brim, Swoop, Hush). The App Store name could be "Snapjaw: Flytrap" so it also captures "flytrap" searches.
2. **Snaptrap**: clean in practice, since the only prior use is likely a delisted 2011 game. It is more literal and says "trap" outright.

Subtitle options (30-character App Store limit):
- "Feed the flytrap. Spare bees." (29)
- "Snap shut. Spare the bees." (26)
- "Fold to snap. Mind the bees." (28) — avoid "Fold to ..." if we want to stay clear of Valley Run's "Fold to Fly" and Fold Smasher's "Fold Your Phone to Smash!"

Pick: **Snapjaw**, subtitle **"Feed the flytrap. Spare bees."**. The subtitle front-loads the flytrap fantasy and the bee rule, which are the two things Fold Smasher does not have.

## Implications for the Trap design

- Lead every store asset with the **plant** and the **bees**, never "smash". Fold Smasher and Squish both own the crush fantasy.
- Make the catch a **partial close**: the lobes meet at some angle above 0°, so slamming flat is wrong or wasteful. This stays clearly distinct from Fold Smasher's flat clap.
- Port the real biology into gameplay: **two trigger-hair touches** before a snap counts, and a slow close lets the fly escape. This gives depth a toy lacks.
- Fold Smasher, ChompDuo and Foldfish are all free with IAP. A £1.99 premium game with real levels and progression is a different shelf, but expect "there's a free one that does this" in reviews.
