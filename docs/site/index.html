Snapjaw

Feed the flytrap. Spare bees.

Hold your iPhone Duo open like a book. It's a Venus flytrap. Bugs land on the nectar to sip; snap the phone a little shut to catch them, then open it again. Wait for a crowd, and never eat a bee. Then grow 8 flytraps, chase goals, keep a Daily streak or relax in the Zen Bog.

How it plays

On iPhone Duo

Hold the Duo open like a book. The two halves are the trap's lobes and the hinge is its midrib. When bugs are sipping inside, snap the phone a little shut, about 22° from however you like to hold it, and the trap bites with you. Then open it again. You never close it or lay it flat.

On other iPhones

Tap anywhere to snap; the trap opens again by itself. Same bog, same bugs, same scores. The fold is the best way to play, but nobody is left out.

Never eat a bee

Bees love nectar too. They land on the same glistening drops as the flies, and they wear a white halo so you can't miss them. A bee in the bite is let go, unharmed and cross, and costs you an extra snap. A wasp stings: two. Sometimes the best move is to wait.

Wait for a crowd

  • Every bug inside the trap is caught in one bite, and your score is the bugs' points × the number of bugs × your chain. Three flies in one bite is worth far more than three bites.
  • Snaps are what you spend. A run starts with 10 (a few more in your first runs), you hold up to 14, and every snap costs one. Catch 3 or more in one bite for a Feast and win a snap back.
  • Catch 5 or more and it's a big FEAST: the bog stops for a beat, gold sparkles burst, the bugs are gulped one by one and two snaps come back.
  • Every clean bite of 2 or more bugs grows the chain, all the way to ×5. A bite on nothing, or on a bee, starts it again.
  • Keep a bug waiting too long and a snap wilts.
  • Sipping bugs are distracted. Flying ones are quick. Close too slowly and the twitchy ones fly off.
  • When the snaps run out, your trap is tired. One more go?

A day in the bog

Every run moves from morning to night, and the bugs change with the light.

MorningHouseflies, gnat swarms and fruit flies.
NoonBluebottles, lines of ants, ladybirds and hopping grasshoppers.
DuskMoths, mosquitoes, darting dragonflies, and wasps.
NightFireflies and crickets, and now and then a rare Golden Scarab.

The Bug Book

16 species, each with its own drawing and a one-line joke, from the humble housefly to the bumblebee. Catch a bug to add its page; bees and wasps go in when you spare them. After a run, a clue tells you which page to hunt for next.

Goals and the Garden

Three goals at a time

Drawn from 39: a 5-bug bite, spare 8 bees in one run, catch a firefly at night, feast before noon and many more. Each one you complete grows your plant and makes way for a new one, and all three in one run earns a bonus. Your plant climbs 12 ranks, from Sprout to Bog Monarch.

Grow 8 flytraps

Classic, Red Dragon, B52, Sawtooth, Shark Tooth, Fused Tooth, Cupped Trap and, for a Bog Monarch only, Golden Glow. Most are named after real Venus flytrap cultivars. Some grow as you rank up, others are won with a special goal. Pick one and it's your trap in every run. Same bite, new look.

Ways to play

  • Feed: endless waves of bugs from morning to night, until your trap is tired.
  • Daily Bog: the same bugs for everyone, every day, and one try that counts. Keep a streak going and share your score. Then practise as much as you like.
  • Zen Bog: no rush, no end. Snaps never run out, nothing wilts, and a bee costs you nothing but your chain. Stop whenever you like; there's no leaderboard.
  • First Bite: a short lesson on your first go that teaches by doing: one fat fly, your first snap, a bee to wait out and your first triple. Replay it from Settings any time.

Made for the Duo's fold

A snap is a short close of about 22° from whatever relaxed book pose you hold, and the bite happens at 22°, so there's nothing to gain by going further. Snapjaw never asks you to close your phone or lay it flat. iPhone Duo softens one half of the screen for a moment when a fold starts from flat, so your score and snaps sit on the half that stays sharp (Settings can move them), and you feel a soft tick in your hands whenever a bug lands. Nothing real ever goes on the screen.

Pay once. That's it.

No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no tracking and no data collected. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Every sound, from the CHOMP to the frogs and the crickets, is made live in the app. Game Center is optional.
