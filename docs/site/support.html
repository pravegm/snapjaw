Playing

How do I play on iPhone Duo?

The phone is the trap: open phone, open trap; closed phone, closed trap. The two halves are the flytrap's lobes and the hinge is its midrib. Hold it any way you like, landscape or portrait, open wide or part way: there's no pose or angle to find. Every round starts with the phone closed: close it, then open it, and the bugs arrive. When bugs are sipping nectar inside, close the phone all the way and the trap bites as the halves meet: CHOMP. While it's shut, the outer screen shows your catch. Open it again to keep feeding. Your first go starts at Level 1, and Levels 1–5 teach one idea each.

How do I play on a normal iPhone?

Tap anywhere to snap: a tap is a bite. The trap opens again by itself a moment later. Every level, every mode and every achievement works this way, and nothing waits for a hinge: a level starts when you tap Go (or anywhere on the screen).

It says "Close the phone to start"

On iPhone Duo every round starts with the trap closed, so the game waits for your phone to close. Close it, then open it: the trap opens with it and the bugs arrive. Any opening plays; it never asks for a particular angle.

Nothing bites when I close it part way

That's by design: only a full close bites. The trap bites as the halves meet, so a half close, however deep, never bites; it just makes the bugs nervous. Close the phone all the way, in one go.

What's the trick?

Patience, then decision. Every bug still inside the trap when it shuts is caught in one bite, and the score is the bugs' points × the number of bugs × your chain, so a crowd is worth waiting for. Bugs sipping nectar (the gold ring) are distracted. Catch 3 or more at once for a Feast and win snaps back. Every clean bite of 2 or more bugs grows your chain, up to ×5; a single bug keeps it where it is, and a bug that gets away starts it again. And never eat a bee.

The bugs flew off before I caught them

The flies feel the lobes coming. Once you start closing, twitchy bugs (houseflies, mosquitoes, dragonflies) notice within a few degrees and fly off a moment later, so a slow, hesitant close loses them (the result says "Too slow, they flew"). Choose your moment, then close in one smooth go. Ants and beetles notice much later. There's no need to close harder: the bite lands as the halves meet, and force is never measured.

I bit a bee

It's let go, unharmed and a bit cross ("Bzzt!"), and it costs you an extra snap; a wasp stings and costs two. It also starts your chain again. Bees, bumblebees and wasps all wear a white halo so you can spot them. When one is sipping in the trap, wait for it to leave.

How do I get more snaps?

An Endless or Daily run starts with 12 snaps (your first three runs start with 14, 13 and 12) and you can hold up to 14. Every bite costs one. A Feast (3 bugs or more in one bite) wins one back, a big FEAST (5 or more) wins two, and a ladybird or the rare Golden Scarab brings luck too. A bite with a bee or wasp in it wins nothing back. If a bug waits in the trap too long, a snap wilts ("Hungry! Bite soon" warns you first). When you're down to 0, your trap is tired and the run ends. A level has its own bites, shown on its card.

Is this OK for my hinge?

Yes. Opening and closing the phone is exactly what the Duo's hinge is made for, and snaps are limited, so a run is only a few dozen closes. The bite lands as the halves meet and force is never measured, so a gentle close catches as much as a hard one: there's never a reason to press or bang it shut. Never put anything real on the screen.

Half the screen went blurry for a moment

That's an iOS effect: when you start folding a Duo from fully flat, iOS briefly blurs one half of the screen for a second or two. It isn't Snapjaw and no app can switch it off. So Snapjaw keeps your score, snaps and hints on the half that stays sharp (the right half), and you feel a tick in your hands whenever a bug lands, wherever it is. If the other half stays sharp on your Duo, use Settings > Score on the left. It only happens when a fold starts from fully flat.

What's on the outer screen when I close it?

When you close the phone, iOS moves Snapjaw to the outer screen, which shows the CHOMP card: the closed trap with your catch, the points and a tip. While it's shut the game waits: no bugs arrive and nothing wilts. Open it again to keep feeding. Closing is never a pause; to pause, use the pause button.

How does the Daily Bog work?

The same bugs for everyone that day. Your first try of the day is the one that counts for the Daily Bog leaderboard; you can practise as much as you like afterwards. A new Daily Bog starts at midnight UTC (midnight UK time in winter).

What's my streak, and what does Share send?

Your streak (🔥) counts the days in a row you've played the Daily Bog's scored try. Home shows it from two days on. After your scored try, Share opens your iPhone's share sheet with one line of text, like "Snapjaw Daily Bog #301 · 4,210 · biggest bite 6 · 🔥 4 days 🪤". You choose where it goes, or cancel; Snapjaw sends nothing itself.

How do goals work?

Once you've passed Level 3, or played a run of Endless, the Daily Bog or the Zen Bog, Home shows three goals, drawn from a pool of 39, such as "A 5-bug bite" or "Spare 8 bees in one run". Goals are played in Endless, the Daily Bog and the Zen Bog, and each says which runs count (such as "Any mode", "Daily" or "Zen"). Progress on a goal you haven't finished is kept between runs. When you complete one, you see "Goal done!", your plant grows, and a new goal takes its place; complete all three in one run for a bonus. The goals get bolder as your plant grows.

The Garden

Home > Garden holds 8 flytraps: Classic, Red Dragon, B52, Sawtooth, Shark Tooth, Fused Tooth, Cupped Trap and Golden Glow. Four arrive with your plant rank (Red Dragon at Nibbler, B52 at Gnat Gobbler, Cupped Trap at Marsh Jaws, Golden Glow at Bog Monarch) and three are won with a special goal, which shows up on Home once your rank is high enough. Tap one to play with it. They only change the look: every flytrap bites exactly the same.

What is the Zen Bog?

A relaxed bog that never ends. Snaps never run out, nothing wilts, and a bee or wasp in the bite costs you no snap (it still ends your chain). The day keeps turning, but the night never speeds up. Bugs you catch fill the Bug Book and count toward goals marked "Zen" or "Any mode", and they grow your plant more slowly than an Endless run. When you've had enough, pause and tap End. There's no leaderboard and no best to beat.

The Bug Book and plant ranks

The Bug Book has 16 species, each with a drawing, a one-line joke and how many you've caught. A page fills in the first time you catch that bug; bees, bumblebees and wasps fill in the first time you spare one. Your plant rank, from Sprout to Bog Monarch, grows with the bugs you catch and the goals you complete. Ranks unlock flytraps for the Garden and bolder goals; they never change the rules. After a run, the results give you a clue to the next page to find. There are no purchases, ever.

How do Levels work?

Home > Levels opens the map: 30 levels in three worlds, Morning Bog (1–10), Dusk (11–20) and Night (21–30). Each level has one clear goal, sometimes with a limit on bites or time, and earns up to three stars for how few bites you used or how cleanly you caught. Levels open in order; 20 stars open Dusk and 40 open the Night. Play on Home always takes you to your next level.

Can I see the lessons again?

Yes. Levels 1–5 teach one idea each; replay any level from the Levels map. Settings also has Replay Level 1.

Sound, feel and settings

No sound, or too much?

Snapjaw plays sound even with the silent switch on. To make it follow the silent switch, turn on Respect silent switch in Settings. Sound and Haptics each have their own switch.

Reduce motion

Settings > Reduce motion turns down the camera bump and screen effects when the trap bites. The bugs and the trap still move: that's the game.

Game Center

Optional. If you're signed in, your best Endless score goes to the Best Score leaderboard, your counted Daily Bog try to the Daily Bog leaderboard, and achievements are reported as you earn them. Levels and the Zen Bog submit no scores. Snapjaw works fully without it.

Purchases and your progress

Will I have to pay again later?

No. One price, no in-app purchases and no ads. Updates are free.

Family Sharing?

Yes, Snapjaw is available through Family Sharing.

Did I lose my progress?

Your progress lives only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone's own backups). Deleting the app deletes it, and we can't restore it, because we never had it.

Reporting a bug

Open an issue with:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings > General > About);
  • whether you were playing with the fold or by tapping;
  • what you were playing (a level and its number, Endless, the Daily Bog or the Zen Bog);
  • what happened, and what you expected (for a bite that fired too early or never fired, whether you had closed the phone all the way);
  • a screen recording, if you can. It helps a lot.
