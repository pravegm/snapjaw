## The pitch (one paragraph)

**Snapjaw turns the iPhone Duo into a Venus flytrap.** Hold the Duo open like a book: the two halves are the trap's lobes and the hinge is its midrib. Bugs fly in and land on the glistening nectar to sip. Snap the phone a little shut and the trap bites with you, teeth clacking together, then open it again and wait. The trick is choosing when: a crowd caught in one bite multiplies your score by the number of bugs in it, but bees love nectar too, and you must never eat a bee. Snaps are what you spend, and big bites earn them back: catch five or more at once and the whole bog stops for a FEAST. Each run passes from morning to night with new bugs, from houseflies at breakfast to dragonflies at dusk and fireflies after dark. Three goals at a time grow your plant from Sprout to Bog Monarch, a Garden of eight flytraps (Red Dragon, B52, Shark Tooth and more) is yours to grow, and a Bug Book of 16 species waits to be filled. There's a Daily Bog with the same bugs for everyone and a streak to keep, a Zen Bog for when you'd rather not count snaps at all, and a First Bite lesson that teaches by doing. On other iPhones, tap to snap. £1.99 / $1.99, no ads, no in-app purchases, no data collected, rated 4+.

## Key features (for writers who want bullets)

- **The phone is the plant.** A real Venus flytrap is two hinged lobes with teeth on the rim. So is an iPhone Duo held open like a book. The halves are the lobes, the hinge is the midrib, and the teeth on screen lean in as your hands close.
- **Choosing, not hitting.** Most of the game is waiting: two flies sipping, a third on the way, and a bee drifting toward the nectar. Bite now, or wait for the crowd? Score = the bugs' points × the number of bugs × the chain.
- **Never eat a bee.** Bees, bumblebees and wasps want the nectar too. A bee in the bite is let go, unharmed and cross ("Bzzt!"), and costs an extra snap; a wasp stings for two. Sparing them is half the skill.
- **Snaps are the resource.** 10 to start (a few more in your first runs), 14 at most, every snap costs one, and a Feast (3 bugs or more in one bite) wins snaps back. When they run out, your trap is tired.
- **The big FEAST.** Five bugs or more in one bite stops the bog: a beat of hit-stop, a gold burst, a big "FEAST!" banner, the bugs gulped into the midrib one after another, two snaps back and a special CHOMP that ends in a burp.
- **Goals and growth.** Three goals at a time, from a pool of 39 ("A 5-bug bite", "Spare 8 bees in one run", "Catch a firefly at night"). Each one grows the plant and makes way for the next, with a bonus for all three in one run.
- **The Garden.** Eight flytraps to grow: Classic, Red Dragon, B52, Sawtooth, Shark Tooth, Fused Tooth, Cupped Trap and, for a Bog Monarch, Golden Glow. Most take their names from real Venus flytrap cultivars. Cosmetic only: same bite, new look.
- **The Daily Bog and the Zen Bog.** The same bugs for everyone each day, with a streak and a one-line Share; or a Zen Bog that never ends, where nothing wilts and there's no leaderboard.
- **A day in the bog.** Every run moves from a gold morning to a blue night, and the cast changes with the light: ant lines and ladybirds by noon, moths and dragonflies at dusk, fireflies and crickets after dark, and now and then a Golden Scarab.
- **Kind to the hinge.** A snap is a short close of about 22° from however you like to hold the Duo. The bite is decided at 22°, so there's nothing to gain by closing further, and the game never asks you to close the phone or lay it flat.
- **It works around the Duo's blur.** iPhone Duo softens one half of the screen for a moment when a fold starts from flat; the score and snaps live on the half that stays sharp, and every landing also ticks in your hands.
- **The CHOMP.** A wet thwack, the teeth clacking together, a squelchy gulp that grows with the crowd, and the strongest haptic the phone has. All synthesised live.
