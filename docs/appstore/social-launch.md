## 3. Video storyboards

### 3A. Social teaser: "Your Duo is a flytrap" (15 s, vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, sound on)

**Shot:** the founder's real iPhone Duo held open like a book in two hands, filmed **from just behind and above the left shoulder**, so the hands, the hinge and both screens are all in frame (the HUD is on the right half, nearest the camera's view). Hold it in a relaxed book, never flat, so the iOS blur never shows. Soft daylight, plain background. Phone sound **on** (clip mic near the speaker). Real hands are fine here (not an App Store asset).

| Time | Picture | On-screen text (big, top third, clear of the phone) | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Cold open: the trap across both halves, a fly landing on a nectar drop with a soft tick | **Your Duo is a flytrap.** | bog bed: frogs, a drip, a buzz |
| 2.0–5.0 | Two more flies land and sip (gold rings). A bee bumbles in toward the nectar. The hands hold still | **Wait for a crowd…** | buzzes, a warm bee hum |
| 5.0–7.5 | The bee lifts off and leaves | **…never eat a bee.** | the hum fading |
| 7.5–10.0 | One short snap: the teeth clack together, the flies are gulped, "TRIPLE ×3" pops, the phone opens again | **CHOMP.** | the thwack, the double clack, the gulp |
| 10.0–12.5 | A quick montage of the light changing: noon, dusk with a dragonfly, night with fireflies glowing | **Morning to night.** | crickets coming in |
| 12.5–15.0 | End card over Home: "Snapjaw" wordmark, "Feed the flytrap. Spare bees.", "Out now on the App Store" | **Snapjaw · out now** | one last little "mm" gulp |

Rules: no price in the video (it goes in the post text); one snap only, short and calm; never closed, never flat, never repeated fast snaps; no squashed-looking bugs. Export H.264, ≤ 60 MB (fits X, Bluesky, Mastodon, Threads, TikTok).

## 5. Don'ts

- No "smash", "squish", "squash", "splat", "kill" or "Fold to …" (Fold Smasher owns the crush fantasy; we're the patient plant).
- No competitor names (Fold Smasher, ChompDuo, Foldfish), not even as "like X but".
- No "break your hinge", "snap as fast as you can" or wear jokes. One calm snap on camera, never repeated fast snaps, never closing the phone shut.
- No bee ever eaten on camera.
- No "share to unlock", follow-for-codes giveaways or bought engagement.
- "iPhone Duo" only in referential use ("for iPhone Duo"), never as part of our product's name; no Apple logos.
- No price inside videos (keep it in the post text).
