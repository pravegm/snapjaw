# Same format as ci/store-scenarios.txt: name|demo args|wait|caption|expect|frame (frame: flat or book).
#  - Not ci/duo-scenarios.txt: that file is duo.yml's review set. This one is the upload set, in screenshots.md's
#    order, with captions.
#  - HUD on the right half (the sharp half, the Duo default): the workflow requires hudLeft=0, so never add hudLeft=1
#    to a D line (DR10 has it, and is FLAGGED by design: review only).
#  - D01 `book`: this capture, split at the fold and drawn on a stylised Duo standing open (store-frame --book): the
#    two lobes on the two pages, the midrib on the hinge. Shot with the real hinge at 140° (`ciHinge=140`, a CI-only
#    key ci/duo.sh strips), so the composite's angle is the logged one (store-set.sh book_angle).
#  - The pilot plays every play frame (input `pilot`, so the Scene plays its own bite; the frames freeze on open-trap
#    moments only: a crowd, a bee, the FEAST pop after the lobes reopen. Never a frame with the lobes drawn shut on
#    the inner screen: on a real Duo they never are, the phone itself closes).
#  - Same glossary and truthfulness notes as ci/store-scenarios.txt. "shut" on its own and "fast" are flagged.
#
# R9 (design/notes/store-R9.md), the R8 game: the phone IS the trap. The set sells it in its first three frames:
#   D01 the Duo is the flytrap (open phone, open trap), D02 close the phone: the outer screen's CHOMP card (the bite's
#   result while the Duo is shut: "CHOMP! n bugs · +points", a bump per bug, "Open to keep feeding"), D03 wait for a
#   crowd: the FEAST. Then the bee, the 30 levels, the night, the Garden, the Bug Book, Zen and the goals.
#  - D02 is `screen=chomp` on the inner display (the hinge open at 150°: the demo forces the cover card onto the
#    panel it is shot from): a real bite of 3+ bugs played headless by the pilot first, so the numbers are real (the
#    expect FLAGS a made-up one: "SNAP-T chomp card catchBite made up"). The card as it shows on the real outer display
#    is DR12 (1398 x 2034, Apple's iPhone Duo OUTER size: not this set's slot, so FLAGGED here by design).
#  - One progress state, `late` (D03, D05, D07–D10), as the iPhone sets.
