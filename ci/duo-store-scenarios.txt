#    the two lobes on the two pages, the midrib on the hinge. The split is geometric, so the layout is kept exactly.
#  - No `fold=`: the Duo has a real one. The pilot plays every play frame (the held hinge never pauses a run).
#  - D02 is an open-after-bite moment (REVIEW-4 #3, #15; Duo P2-3), not the hold or the release: `ciShootOn=feast`
#    (a CI-only key, stripped by ci/duo.sh) shoots the live run CI_SHOOT_DELAY (0.5) s after `SNAP-T event feast`,

#    after the bite: the dark, narrow release that read as "a plum".)
#  - Same glossary and truthfulness notes as ci/store-scenarios.txt. "Snap it a little shut" is fine; "shut" on its
#    own is flagged.
